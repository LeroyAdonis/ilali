# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-08-18

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Cape Town's trusted place to find safe, vetted extramural activities for your kids.

**What it does:** ILALI is a two-sided marketplace connecting Cape Town families with children's extramural activity providers. Parents search, filter, and discover vetted activities by age, category, location, and price. Providers list their activities, earn trust badges, and reach families who are actively looking. An AI match feature lets parents describe what they need in plain language ("find soccer for my 8-year-old in Claremont") and get tailored results.

**Product category:** Children's activities / extramurals discovery and listing platform

**Product type:** Two-sided marketplace (parents free, providers subscription + commission)

**Business model:**
- **Parents:** Free to browse, search, save, and contact providers. No booking fees.
- **Providers:** R99/month (first 30 days free) + 10% commission on online bookings once live. Pricing is env-configurable for partner deals (e.g. Assitej SA onboarding Sept 2026).
- **MVP reality:** Online booking/payments are WS-6 (post-launch). Effective MVP charge is the R99 subscription only. Parents currently contact providers via WhatsApp.

## Target Audience
**Two-sided marketplace — two distinct audiences:**

### Parents (demand side)
- **Who:** Moms and dads in Cape Town suburbs, typically 30s–40s
- **Tech level:** Non-tech-savvy to moderate; many manage kids' schedules via WhatsApp groups and Facebook posts
- **Mindset:** Trust-first — child safety is the #1 concern. Want background-checked providers before committing.
- **Location:** Cape Town metro, filtering by suburb (Claremont, Pinelands, Observatory, Gardens, etc.)
- **Kids:** Ages 3–17, usually one or two children in the 4–12 range
- **Budget:** Price-sensitive in Rands; compare R200/week football vs R180/week coding club

### Providers (supply side)
- **Who:** Small activity businesses, independent coaches, studios, and holiday program operators
- **Size:** Often solo founders or 2–3 person operations
- **Tech level:** Non-tech-savvy; many run their business entirely via WhatsApp and word-of-mouth
- **Pain:** No online discoverability, admin chaos from WhatsApp threads, no trust signal to stand out
- **Goal:** Fill spots, get more families, look professional without building a website

**Primary use case:** A parent finds a safe, age-appropriate activity for their child in their suburb. A provider gets listed and reaches families actively searching.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Find a vetted activity for my child that fits our schedule, location, and budget
- Feel confident the provider is safe and background-checked before signing up
- Get my activity listed so more local families can find me

**Use cases:**
- Sarah (mom, 7-year-old): "I need something for Aisha on Tuesday afternoons in Claremont — she likes art"
- Thabo (dance studio owner): "I want more students but I don't know how to reach parents outside my WhatsApp group"
- Weekend holiday program search during school breaks
- After-school activity discovery at start of a new school term

## Problems & Pain Points

### For Parents
**Core problem:** "I don't know how to find safe, good activities for my kid without asking around in WhatsApp groups and hoping for the best."

**Why current solutions fall short:**
- Facebook groups are chaotic, unsearchable, and unvetted — anyone can post anything
- Word-of-mouth only works within your immediate network — limited to what your friends know
- Google searches surface national chains, not local Cape Town coaches
- No way to verify if a provider is safe, background-checked, or legitimate
- Every parent reinvents the wheel each school term

**What it costs them:**
- Hours scrolling Facebook groups and asking friends for recommendations
- Anxiety about whether a provider is safe ("How do I know this person is who they say they are?")
- Kids missing out on activities because parents couldn't find them in time
- Inconsistent information across different providers and platforms

**Emotional tension:**
- Fear: "Am I putting my child in safe hands?"
- Overwhelm: "There are too many options and no way to compare"
- Frustration: "Why is this so hard to find?"
- Guilt: "Other moms seem to have it figured out — what am I missing?"

### For Providers
**Core problem:** "I'm great at what I do, but I can't get more families to find me."

**Why current solutions fall short:**
- WhatsApp groups are informal, unsearchable, and expire
- Word-of-mouth caps out quickly — you only reach people who already know you
- Facebook pages require ad spend to reach anyone new
- No trust signal — parents have no way to know you're legitimate
- Admin takes over: chasing payments, managing schedules, answering the same questions

**What it costs them:**
- Empty class spots that could be filled
- Time wasted on admin instead of teaching
- Inability to grow beyond their immediate network
- Looking unprofessional compared to bigger studios

**Emotional tension:**
- "I'm invisible online"
- "Parents don't trust new providers"
- "I spend more time on WhatsApp than on my actual craft"

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** No direct Cape Town-specific children's activity marketplace with progressive vetting and AI match exists yet. ILALI is creating the category locally.

**Secondary competitors (different solution, same problem):**
- **Facebook Groups** (e.g. "Cape Town Moms", suburb-specific groups): Free, familiar, huge reach — but chaotic, unvetted, unsearchable, and noisy. Providers can't build a professional presence. Parents can't filter or verify.
- **WhatsApp community groups:** Intimate and trusted within the group — but ephemeral, unsearchable, and limited to the group's network. Information gets lost. No trust signal.
- **Google search:** Comprehensive but unfiltered — surfaces national chains, outdated listings, and irrelevant results. No local-first filtering. No vetting.
- **Activity-specific platforms (e.g. Kidspot, FunKidsSA):** National scope, often outdated listings, no Cape Town local focus, no progressive verification, no AI match.

**Indirect competitors:**
- **Individual provider websites:** Providers who've built their own sites have an advantage — but most small operators can't or won't do this.
- **School newsletters/word-of-mouth:** Trusted but limited to one school's network. Not scalable.

**How each falls short:** None combine Cape Town local focus + progressive trust badges + AI-powered discovery + mobile-first design + community-powered growth. Facebook and WhatsApp are the status quo — convenient but unsafe and disorganized.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- **Progressive verification badges:** Listed (grey, instant) → Verified (teal, docs) → Trusted (gold, vouches + reviews). Nothing unlabelled. Parents see trust at a glance.
- **AI match:** Natural language search ("find art classes for my 5-year-old in Gardens") — parents don't need to know categories or filters.
- **Community-powered growth:** School partnerships, WhatsApp group integration, community referrals — not paid ads. The platform grows like a village, not a business.
- **AI poster onboarding:** Providers send a WhatsApp poster/flyer and the AI extracts and builds their listing. Zero tech friction.
- **Cape Town local-first:** Every page is designed for Cape Town suburbs, Rands pricing, school terms, and SA context.
- **No booking fees for parents:** Browse, search, save, and contact — always free.

**How we do it differently:** We don't just list activities — we verify the people behind them, use AI to make discovery effortless, and grow through the community itself rather than ad spend.

**Why that's better:** Parents save time and feel safe. Providers get discovered and look professional. The community benefits from transparency and trust.

**Why customers choose us:** Safety (background checks are non-negotiable), simplicity (find an activity in 30 seconds), and locality (Cape Town specific, not national).

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "How do I know the providers are actually safe?" | Every provider is background-checked. We verify police clearances, ID documents, and business details. Trust badges show exactly where a provider is in the verification process — nothing is hidden. |
| "I can just use Facebook groups — they're free" | Facebook groups are free but unvetted and unsearchable. You're relying on strangers' recommendations with no verification. ILALI gives you the same local recommendations but with background checks, age filters, and suburb search. |
| "I'm too busy / not tech-savvy enough for this" | That's exactly who we built this for. You don't need an app — just search on your phone, filter by age and suburb, and find something. For providers: describe your activity and our AI builds your listing for you. |
| "What does it cost?" | For parents: completely free, forever. For providers: R99/month with the first 30 days free. No setup fees, no hidden costs. |
| "I already have enough families through word-of-mouth" | Word-of-mouth is great — until you have empty spots. ILALI fills the gaps between your existing network and the families actively searching right now. |

**Anti-personas:**
- **National chains** looking for ad placement or lead generation — we're Cape Town local, not a national directory.
- **Brands wanting paid advertising** on the platform — our growth is community-powered, not ad-driven.
- **Non-Cape-Town providers** — ILALI is intentionally local. We don't serve other cities (yet).
- **Providers unwilling to be background-checked** — trust is non-negotiable. If you won't verify, you won't list.

- Providers: invisible online, admin chaos, can't grow beyond WhatsApp network

**Pull (what attracts them to ILALI):**

- Providers: professional listing in minutes, trust badges, dashboard, reach new families

**Habit (what keeps them stuck):**

**Anxiety (what worries them about switching):**

|------|---------|
