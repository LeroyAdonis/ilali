# ILALI Update for George — Making ILALI Easier for Parents & Providers

**From:** Leroy's team
**Date:** 13 August 2026
**What this is:** A short, plain-language update on the work we're doing to ILALI before the September launch. No technical jargon — promise. 😊

---

## The short version

We're making ILALI **easier to use for everyone** — the parents looking for activities, and the providers (the dance teachers, coaches, holiday-programme organisers) who list on it. The goal: **less form-filling, less waiting, fewer confusing steps.** People should feel looked after at every stage.

## What changes for parents

- **No more signing up just to look.** Right now a parent has to create an account before browsing. After this change, anyone can browse, search, and use the smart match tool **without an account**.
- **"Keep me posted" instead of a password.** When a parent saves an activity or contacts a provider, they just pop in their email and we send them a link — no passwords to invent, no "at least 8 characters" walls.
- **We keep them in the loop automatically.** They get a friendly note when they save something, a confirmation when they book, a reminder the day before the class ("bring a smock 🎨"), and a nudge after to say how it went. Quiet parents get a gentle monthly "new things for your kids near you" — never spam.

**Why we made this call:** This is what the big players overseas do (ClassForKids is used by almost half of UK parents and their secret is exactly this — browse first, sign up only when you actually want to do something). For South African parents, where a lot of us live on WhatsApp, the easier we make it, the more families actually use it.

## What changes for providers

- **No more temporary passwords.** Right now when we approve a provider, the system creates a random password and emails it to them — it's confusing and people get stuck. Instead, they'll get a **magic link** (one tap, and they're in).
- **A simple 4-step setup wizard.** Instead of one long form, it's one friendly screen at a time, with a preview at the end showing exactly what parents will see.
- **They always know where things stand.** After they submit, they see a simple tracker: *Draft → Submitted → Under review → Live* — with a promise of "usually reviewed within 24-48 hours". No more wondering.

**Why we made this call:** Providers are busy people running small businesses. Every extra step between "I want to be listed" and "I'm live" is a provider we lose. The Assitej launch is exactly where this matters — the less friction, the more providers we onboard in September.

## The good news for you specifically

1. **Your poster process stays exactly as is.** We know you wanted the poster upload flow kept, and it's staying. You drop a poster in, we read it, we create the provider profile — unchanged. ✅
2. **The pricing on the website stays as is too.** R99/month (first 30 days free) + 10% commission on online bookings. Parents never pay. We've just made sure it's set up so that **when Assitej terms are agreed, we can switch them on with a flick of a setting** — no rebuild, no waiting. You tell us the deal, we apply it.
3. **The tech side is Leroy's department** — you don't need to worry about any of it. We'll keep you posted as things go live.
